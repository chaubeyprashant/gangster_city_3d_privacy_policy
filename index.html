1. Introduction & Scope

Ergebins Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices concerning data collection, use, and disclosure through your download, installation, and gameplay of Gangster City 3D on Android devices.

Core Commitment: Gangster City 3D is designed to be enjoyed offline and locally. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party data brokers.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) directly from users. Specifically, we do NOT ask for or collect:

  • Your real name, postal address, or telephone number
  • Email addresses or contact lists
  • Precise GPS location or background location data
  • Camera, microphone, photos, media, or SMS data
  • Payment card details, bank account numbers, or financial account credentials
  • Biometric data or facial scans

3. Information That May Be Collected Automatically

When you run the game on an Android device, certain non-identifying technical information and diagnostics may be processed automatically to ensure technical stability, proper rendering, and error resolution:

Data Category Description Purpose
Device Specifications Device model, CPU/GPU architecture (ARM64, Vulkan/OpenGL ES3 support), RAM capacity, screen resolution, and OS version. To select optimal graphics quality presets (texture resolution, shadow tier, frame rate target) and verify hardware compatibility.
Diagnostic & Crash Data Application crash logs, stack traces, and rendering pipeline exceptions generated during runtime. To identify software defects, fix bugs in upcoming updates, and enhance performance across various Android devices.
Gameplay Telemetry Aggregate performance metrics (frame rates, 1% low FPS, memory usage counters). To benchmark performance optimization and prevent frame drops during gameplay.

4. Local Game Storage & Saves

All game progression data—including mission states, unlocked vehicles, collected items, inventory, weapon loadouts, and audio/graphics settings—is stored locally on your device's internal storage sandbox using standard Unity serialization (e.g., Application.persistentDataPath).

This data stays on your personal device. If you uninstall the application or clear application data via Android device settings, your local save data will be permanently removed.

5. Android App Permissions

Gangster City 3D adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege. The application does not declare any dangerous runtime permissions. The manifest permissions utilized are:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Enables network transmission for anonymous engine error reporting, standard package manager verification, or Google Play licensing services if applicable. The core game experience remains fully playable offline.

6. Third-Party Libraries & Services

The game is built using the Unity 6 engine and distributed via the Google Play Store. These platform providers may process non-personal diagnostic data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Provided by Google LLC for app distribution, automated integrity checks, and Android platform compatibility. For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Technologies: The game engine runtime may collect anonymous hardware information and crash telemetry. For more information, review the Unity Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K Compliance)

Gangster City 3D contains stylized 3D action gameplay and is intended for a general gaming audience (age 13+ or as specified by Google Play content ratings in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us without your consent, please contact us immediately so that we can take corrective action.

8. User Rights & Choices (GDPR / CCPA)

Under international privacy regulations (including GDPR in Europe and CCPA/CPRA in California), you have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Portability: You may request information about any data we process concerning you. Because we do not collect personal accounts or server-side profiles, your game data is stored directly on your device.
  • Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): You can delete all local data associated with Gangster City 3D at any time by clearing application data in Android Settings > Apps > Gangster City 3D > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell your personal information or device data to any third party.

9. Data Security & Retention

We take appropriate administrative and technical precautions to safeguard device data. Because we do not operate remote databases storing user identities or personal records, your exposure to remote data breaches from our systems is minimized.

Any updates will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of the document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
