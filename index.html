1. Introduction & Scope

Ergebins Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used and what choices you have when you download, install and play Gangster City 3D on Android devices.

The short version: you sign in (with Google, an email address, or as a guest) before you play. The game uses Google Firebase for sign-in, crash reports and usage statistics, and Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and an occasional full-screen ad. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Sign-In & Account Information

The game asks you to sign in before you play, using Google Firebase Authentication. You choose one of three ways:

  • Google: we receive your Google account's user ID, display name, email address and profile photo link.
  • Email and password: we receive your email address. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
  • Guest: an anonymous account ID is created. No name or email address is collected.

We use this to identify your account in the game and to keep one account per player. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, SMS messages, biometric data or payment card details.

3. Information Collected Automatically

While you play, the following information is processed automatically:

Data Category Description Purpose
Device Specifications Device model, CPU/GPU, RAM, screen resolution and Android version. To choose graphics quality and frame-rate settings that suit your phone.
Crash & Diagnostic Data Crash logs and stack traces (Google Firebase Crashlytics). To find and fix bugs and improve stability.
Gameplay Analytics Events such as missions started, completed or failed, chapters, story choices, level-ups, wanted-level peaks, arrests, deaths, and one performance sample (frame rate, memory, graphics setting) per session (Google Firebase Analytics, linked to an app-instance ID, not your name). To understand how the game is played and where it needs improving.
Advertising Data The Android advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions (Google AdMob). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

Crash and usage reporting can be switched off at any time in the game under Settings > Share usage & crash data.

4. Game Progress & Local Storage

Your game progress (mission states, unlocked vehicles and weapons, collected items, inventory, cash, upgrades and audio/graphics settings) is saved on your device using standard Unity storage (Application.persistentDataPath). We do not currently upload your game progress to our servers.

If you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings, this local save is permanently removed.

5. Android App Permissions

The game declares no dangerous runtime permissions. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. The permissions it uses are:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: sign-in, crash reports, analytics and ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*: lets Google AdMob read the advertising ID and Android's ad services to show and measure ads.
  • WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and Google Play install-referrer / services access: used by Google's Firebase and advertising libraries to run reliably.

6. Third-Party Services

The game is built with the Unity 6 engine and uses the following services, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics): sign-in, usage statistics and crash reports. See the Firebase privacy information.
  • Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads and occasional full-screen ads. In the EU, UK and Switzerland a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform asks about ad data use before any ad is requested. See how Google uses data in AdMob.
  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In: app distribution, integrity checks and Google account sign-in. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Technologies: the engine runtime may collect anonymous hardware information and crash telemetry. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Watching a rewarded ad is always your choice: it is offered for a bonus (such as cash, health, ammo or a weapon unlock) and the game never requires it to play.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K Compliance)

Gangster City 3D contains stylized crime-action content and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has signed in or provided information, please contact us and we will delete the account and its data.

8. Your Rights & Choices (GDPR / CCPA)

  • Access & portability: email us to ask what we hold about your account.
  • Deletion: email us and we will delete your account and its associated Firebase data within 30 days. You can also delete all local game data by clearing app storage in Android Settings > Apps > Gangster City 3D > Storage, or by uninstalling the game.
  • Usage and crash data: switch it off in the game under Settings > Share usage & crash data.
  • Ads: rewarded ads are optional. Where the consent form appears, your choice is respected; you can contact us to withdraw it. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Sign out: from the Account screen in the game's main menu.
  • Opt-out of sale: we do not sell your personal information.

9. Data Security & Retention

Data is sent to Google's services over encrypted connections. Account information is kept until you ask us to delete it; analytics, crash and advertising data are kept by Google as described in their policies. We take appropriate precautions to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
